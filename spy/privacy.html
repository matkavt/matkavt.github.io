Privacy Policy — Spy: Find the Imposter

Last updated: October 2026

Spy ("the app") is made by Matvey Kavtorov, an independent developer. This policy explains what data the app processes on iPhone, iPad and Android, why, and what choices you have. Questions and requests: matkavt@gmail.com.

No accounts

You don't create an account, and the app doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number. Player names you type for a game on one phone stay on your device.

Online games

When you host or join an online game, the app signs in anonymously to Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database). A room holds a random room code, the nicknames players enter, the game settings and the dealt roles. A room is deleted when the host closes it, and rooms left open are removed automatically within about 13 hours.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and A/B Testing to see how the game is used, fix crashes and test improvements. This includes in-app events (for example, a game started or a purchase completed), device model, operating system and app version, country, and app-specific identifiers such as the Firebase installation ID and, on iOS, the identifier for vendor. It does not include your name or contact details.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through the Appodeal mediation platform and its advertising and measurement partners (for example AppLovin, Unity Ads, Liftoff/Vungle, Mintegral, ironSource, InMobi, BidMachine, Amazon, Pangle, Yandex, myTarget, Adjust and AppsFlyer). They may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device information and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. Appodeal's policy: https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/

On iPhone and iPad the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent to personalized ads. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. A subscription or Premium removes ads.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app never sees your payment details; it only learns whether a purchase or subscription is active.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging with a device token to send occasional game reminders. You can turn them off in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Retention and your rights

Analytics and crash data are kept for a limited period set in the providers' retention settings and then deleted. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask for access to or deletion of data linked to your device by writing to matkavt@gmail.com. You can also turn off tracking, reset your advertising identifier or uninstall the app, which stops all collection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
